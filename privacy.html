Privacy Policy - Simple All in One

Last updated: 2026-10-12 · Publisher: Siru · Contact: xians721@gmail.com

What this app is

Simple All in One adds dashboard gadgets (Simple Issue Board, Simple Kanban, Simple Timeline) to Jira Cloud. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform.

What we store

  • Team names and the Atlassian account IDs of the members an administrator adds to a team, with the time each was added.
  • Gadget settings (project key, filters, column layout) are stored by Jira in your dashboard configuration.

What we do not store

We do not store users' names, email addresses, avatars, issue content or credentials. Display names and avatars are retrieved from Jira each time they are shown, using the viewing user's own permissions.

Where data is kept

In Atlassian Forge storage, in the region Atlassian assigns to your Jira site. We operate no servers of our own, and we do not send data to third parties. The only external resource is a set of static gadget thumbnail images hosted on GitHub Pages; loading them involves no personal data from the app.

Retention and deletion

Team data is kept until an administrator changes it or the app is uninstalled; Forge storage is then removed with the app. The app reports the stored account IDs to Atlassian's Personal Data Reporting API at least weekly and removes accounts that Atlassian reports as closed.

Your rights

Because the app stores only account IDs on your Jira site, requests concerning personal data are best directed to your Jira site administrator. You may also contact us at xians721@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when our practices change.
