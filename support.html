Reporting a problem

Please include: the gadget name, your Jira site URL, the project key, what you expected vs. what happened, and a screenshot (hide any information you do not want to share).

Quick answers

  • The gadget says "No project set". Open the gadget's menu, choose Edit, and enter the project key.
  • I cannot save teams. Only Jira administrators can change teams (Settings → Apps → Simple All in One).
  • The Team filter is empty. Create teams on the admin page first, then refresh the dashboard.
  • Moving a card fails. The issue's workflow may not allow that transition, or the transition has required fields; change it on the Jira issue page.
  • Dates cannot be changed on the timeline. The issue type's screen may not include the start/due date field you selected.

Known limitations

  • Korean public holidays are the only built-in holiday calendar (optional; default is weekends only).
  • Priority highlighting assumes Jira's default priority names (Highest ... Lowest).
